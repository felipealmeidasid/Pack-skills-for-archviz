# Architectural Detailing Menu

Use this reference to choose professional detailing topics for interiors, exteriors, architectural storyboard boards, executive presentation boards, and prompt annotations.

Do not generate fake construction documentation unless the user asks for technical drawing language. For AI storyboard purposes, use these as annotation categories, prompt constraints, and professional presentation callouts.

## Interior Detailing

### General Documentation Views

- Internal elevations wall to wall: TV panels, millwork, headboards, kitchens, laundry rooms, bathrooms, halls, home offices.
- Internal sections: longitudinal, transverse, by room, by element, through millwork, ceiling, counters, or cabinets.
- Technical plans: layout, demolition/construction, axes, levels.
- Orthographic axonometrics: full room, by system, millwork, ceiling, lighting, exploded axon.

### Millwork And Fixed Furniture

- Module construction: boxes, dividers, shelves, niches, drawers, lift-up doors.
- Doors and fronts: alignments, panel layout, technical gaps, pulls, recessed pulls, profiles.
- Hinges and slides: position, setbacks, opening direction, interference checks.
- Baseboards, plinths, toe-kicks, and recesses: finishes, level changes, floor junctions.
- Panels: slatted panels, boiserie, wainscoting, TV panels, hidden doors.
- Glass cabinets and shelving: uprights, shelves, embedded lighting, glass/metalwork.
- Closets/wardrobes: hanging rails, trouser racks, drawer units, upper storage, internal lighting.
- Kitchens: modules, hot tower, pantry, wet channels, bins, corners, gola profiles.

### Countertops, Tops, And Vertical Cladding

- Countertop details: thickness, edge, apron, overhang, supports, reinforcement.
- Sink/faucet/accessories: drilling, position, inset, overmount, undermount.
- Backsplash/rodabanca: height, junctions, corners, drips.
- Material transitions: stone-wood, stone-glass, metal-lacquer, finish transitions.
- Thresholds and sills: layout, drip edge, junction with window/door frames.

### Wet Areas

- Shower/box: height, tile layout, linear/point drain, slope indication.
- Shower niche: dimensions, position, edging, waterproofing logic.
- Bathroom countertop: backsplash, basin, mirror, cabinet, lighting.
- Toilet: axis, plumbing point, flush height, accessories.
- Laundry: sink, washing machine, millwork, technical points, ventilation.

### Ceiling, Cove, And Lighting

- Ceiling plan: levels, drops, curtain pockets, coves, linear slots.
- Cove/curtain pocket detail: ceiling section, heights, recesses, fits.
- Lighting plan: spots, linear profiles, pendants, sconces, tracks.
- Recessed fixture detail: slot, technical niche, setbacks, alignments.
- Lighting scenes and zoning when applicable.

### Electrical, Data, And Automation

- Electrical plan: outlets, switches, dedicated use points, heights in centimeters.
- TV/data points: router, HDMI, cable passages, cable trays, shafts.
- Automation panel/center: points, reserves, labels.
- Millwork conflicts: outlets inside cabinets, embedded channels, cable pass-throughs.

### Hydraulic And Gas

- Hydraulic points: cold/hot water, sewage, vent, registers.
- Sink/drain details: position, inspection, technical access.
- Gas: points and passages when relevant.

### Floors, Layout, And Transitions

- Floor layout/pagination: joints, direction, cuts, modulation.
- Floor transition detail: profiles, level changes, expansion.
- Baseboard/chair rail: heights, returns, corners, junction with jambs.

### Wall Cladding And Vertical Layout

- Wall finish maps: modulation, layout, axes, alignments.
- Corner and edge details: trim pieces, profiles, finishes.
- Joints: position, repetition, compatibility with counters and niches.

### Doors, Windows, And Metalwork

- Door detail: jamb, casing, hardware, swing direction.
- Hidden/pivot doors: alignments, gaps, pivot axis.
- Windows: sill, curtain pocket, junction with ceiling and cladding.
- Metalwork: profiles, fits, fixings, glass, partitions.

### Glass, Mirrors, And Shower Box

- Mirror detail: setbacks, spacers, LED, hidden outlet.
- Shower box: fixings, silicone, profile, handle, seals.
- Guardrails/partitions: top/bottom fixing, layout.

### Loose Furniture When Documented

- Elevation/axonometric composition: sofa, tables, chairs, layout and distances.
- Positioning details: rug, coffee table, circulation, alignments with millwork.

### Coordination And Executive Boards

- Interferences: HVAC vs ceiling, millwork vs electrical/hydraulic.
- Axes, levels, references, and construction marks.
- Legends and material/finish schedules for presentation boards.

## Exterior Detailing

### General Exterior Views

- Facade elevations: front, rear, sides with materials, layout, dimensions.
- Exterior sections: parapet, eaves, canopies, balconies, pergolas.
- Orthographic axonometrics of exterior volume and systems.
- Site/implantation plan: access, levels, external areas.
- Roof plan: slopes, flashing, gutters, drains, drainage points.

### Facade And Exterior Cladding

- Cladding layout: stone, porcelain, ACM, exterior wood, cementitious panels, texture/paint.
- Corners and edges: profiles, drip edges, finishes.
- Expansion and control joints: position, repetition, compatibility.
- Material transitions: cladding-paint, stone-metal, wood-glass.
- Thresholds, sills, and drip edges.

### Exterior Openings

- Window/door installation: lintel, sill, sealing, membranes.
- Junction between frame and cladding.
- Entrance doors: jambs, hardware, thresholds, bottom seal.
- Brises and shutters: fixing, modulation, setbacks.

### Roof, Waterproofing, And Rainwater Drainage

- Parapet detail: flashing, top finish, drip edge.
- Gutters and flashings: sections, seams, downspouts.
- Drains and rainwater outlets.
- Slope representation in plan/section.
- Critical junctions: wall-slab, slab-planter, canopy-facade.
- Exterior stairs and landings: waterproofing, slopes, drains.

### Balconies, Terraces, And Canopies

- Exterior floor layers: slope, joints, drain.
- Guardrail detail: top/lateral fixing, finishes, sealing.
- Eaves/canopies: section with drip, underside finish, lighting.
- Exterior baseboards and junctions.

### Decks, Pergolas, And Light Structures

- Wood/composite deck: substructure, spacing, fixing, ventilation.
- Pergola: modulation, supports, connections, hidden gutters when relevant.
- Light covers: glass/polycarbonate/awnings, slope, flashing, drainage.
- Fixing to masonry/metal/concrete: plates, screws, bases.

### Walls, Railings, Gates, And Closures

- Wall detail: coping, drip edge, texture/paint, joints.
- Railings and metalwork: profiles, spacing, fixing.
- Gates: rail, rollers, automation, stops, closures.
- Slatted closures: modulation, fixing, spacing, top/base.

### Landscape And Exterior Areas

- Exterior floor layout: stone, pavers, concrete, pebbles.
- Edge and containment details: curbs, guides, metal profiles.
- Planters: layers, waterproofing, drainage, ventilation.
- Planting beds: edges, levels, wall protection.
- Exterior stairs: treads/risers, anti-slip, nearby drains.
- Paths and ramps: slope, joints, surface drainage.

### Pool, Spa, And Exterior Wet Areas

- Pool edge: infinity edge, coping stone, drip edge.
- Sun deck/solarium: floor, slopes, drains.
- Machine room: technical access, ventilation, passages.
- Drainage: channels, linear drains, runoff.

### Exterior Lighting And Electrical

- Exterior lighting plan: bollards, sconces, recessed lights, posts.
- Floor recessed lights: niche, sealing, drainage.
- Exterior outlets: weatherproof boxes, heights.
- Automation and lighting scenes by zone: garden, facade, circulation.

### Exterior Comfort Complements

- Outdoor gourmet/kitchen: countertops, sink, hood/duct, niches.
- Outdoor fireplace: base, protection, clearances, finishes.
- Technical shelters: gas, HVAC condensers, trash: doors, ventilation, drainage.
- Solar protections: brises, shade screens, fixing, modulation.

## Most Common Exterior Executive Package

For exterior projects, prioritize:

- facades;
- critical sections;
- roof/drainage;
- openings;
- cladding layout;
- waterproofing details;
- balconies/guardrails;
- exterior floors;
- walls/gates;
- exterior lighting.

## How To Use In Storyboards

For each approved image, choose 3 to 7 relevant detail categories. Add them as professional callouts rather than pretending to have final construction documents.

Example:

```text
Technical callouts: facade stone pagination, recessed glass frame, balcony guardrail fixing logic, warm soffit lighting, landscape planter drainage, exterior floor slope toward linear drain.
```

Use this menu to make storyboard boards feel like they were prepared by an experienced architect, not just an image generator.
