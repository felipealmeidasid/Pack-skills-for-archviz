# Resort / Wellness Residencial

## Essence

Residential or hospitality world where home, leisure, wellness, water, garden, service, and calm rituals merge into a premium lifestyle.

## Architecture

Pool, spa, terrace, lounge, restaurant/gourmet, garden path, sauna, massage room, fitness/wellness area. Shaded circulation and soft transitions. Materials should feel tactile under bare feet and hands. Water must behave physically: reflection, ripples, edge, wet stone, soft light.

## Materials

Natural stone, warm wood, linen/towels, ceramic/tableware, matte metal, soft indirect lighting, garden species with climate logic.

## Human Behavior

Walking from pool to shaded lounge, staff setting towels or water, resident drinking coffee after spa, couple entering restaurant terrace, person stretching/yoga in morning light, family using pool naturally.
