# Prompt Rules

## Use Terms Like

Residential resort atmosphere, pool and shaded lounge continuity, spa-like calm, natural stone underfoot, warm wood pergola, white towels and linen, garden path, soft indirect lighting, quiet service gesture, water reflections and subtle ripples, slow observational camera movement.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a cinematic residential resort wellness scene: a pool and shaded lounge connected by natural stone flooring, warm wood pergola, soft towels, dense but controlled landscaping, subtle water reflections, and calm service gestures. Use morning or late-afternoon natural light, tactile materials, relaxed Brazilian premium lifestyle, slow observational camera language, and refined real estate campaign realism. Avoid fake resort cliches, over-saturated sunsets, neon pool lighting, stock-photo posing, plastic CGI, and fantasy villa aesthetics.
```

## Continuity Anchors

Natural stone underfoot, pool reflection, warm wood pergola, linen/towels, garden path, soft service gesture, warm restaurant/spa lighting.
