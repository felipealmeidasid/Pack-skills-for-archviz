# Avoid

- American generic mansion language.
- Ultra-luxury palace vocabulary.
- Fantasy villas and impossible cantilevers.
- Glass walls exposed to harsh tropical sun with no shading.
- Fake palm trees as decoration.
- Over-polished marble everywhere.
- Cold showroom minimalism without climate or human logic.
- Over-saturated sunset and neon pool light.
- Copying a single architect, project, facade, roof, or plan.
