# Prompt Rules

## Use Terms Like

- contemporary Brazilian tropical residence;
- low horizontal massing;
- deep shaded terraces;
- warm wood soffits;
- natural stone flooring;
- protected glass planes;
- indoor-outdoor continuity;
- dense but controlled tropical landscaping;
- pool as reflective cooling plane;
- barefoot premium lifestyle;
- realistic tropical shade;
- physically plausible glass reflections;
- refined commercial archviz realism.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a cinematic real estate image for a contemporary Brazilian tropical residence: low horizontal massing, deep shaded terraces, warm wood soffits, natural stone flooring, protected glass planes, dense tropical landscaping, and a reflective pool connecting living area and garden. Use realistic tropical shade, natural warm light, believable Brazilian premium lifestyle, physically plausible materials, restrained camera language, and refined commercial archviz realism. Avoid fantasy geometry, anime aesthetics, fake luxury, plastic CGI, impossible glass, decorative palm cliches, and stock-photo posing.
```

## Continuity Anchors

Warm wood soffit, natural stone floor, shaded terrace, dense green garden, pool reflection, protected glass, linen wardrobe, black/bronze metal accents.
