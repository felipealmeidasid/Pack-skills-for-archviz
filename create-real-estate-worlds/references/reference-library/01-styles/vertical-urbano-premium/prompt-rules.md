# Prompt Rules

## Use Terms Like

Premium urban residential tower, strong facade rhythm, private balconies with integrated greenery, warm lobby arrival sequence, bronze metal, natural stone, high-performance glass, blue-hour skyline reflections, rooftop pool as emotional payoff, calm concierge gesture, refined urban lifestyle, realistic city context.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a cinematic real estate key visual for a premium urban residential tower: strong vertical facade rhythm, private balconies with integrated greenery, bronze metal accents, natural stone podium, high-performance glass with realistic city reflections, warm lobby glow, and a rooftop pool as the emotional payoff. Use blue-hour atmosphere, believable urban Brazilian premium lifestyle, accurate scale, refined commercial archviz realism, and restrained camera language. Avoid fake skyline, Dubai-style excess, random luxury props, stock-photo posing, impossible glass reflections, and over-rendered CGI.
```

## Continuity Anchors

Facade rhythm, balcony greenery, bronze/dark metal, stone podium, warm lobby light, rooftop pool edge, skyline reflection, concierge/resident arrival gesture.
