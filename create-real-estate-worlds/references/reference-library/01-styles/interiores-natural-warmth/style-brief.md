# Interiores Natural Warmth

## Essence

Warm contemporary interiors built around natural light, wood, stone, neutral fabrics, integrated social spaces, tactile materials, proportion, calm, and believable living.

## Architecture

- Integrated kitchen, dining, living, balcony, or landscape view.
- Floor-to-ceiling glass only when view and light make sense.
- Clean volumes with tactile surfaces.
- Lighting layers: daylight, linear embedded lights, wall washing, accent fixtures.

## Materials

Warm natural wood, large-scale stone with organic veining, soft neutral fabrics, earthy tones, greys, sand, cream, muted greens, matte metals, ceramic, linen, and leather used sparingly.

## Human Behavior

Reading, cooking, setting table, walking through hallway, touching stone or wood, opening curtains, hosting quietly. Avoid fashion editorial posing.
