# Prompt Rules

## Use Terms Like

Contemporary warm residential interior, natural wood with warm undertones, large-scale stone with organic veining, soft neutral fabrics, filtered daylight, integrated kitchen/dining/living, layered lighting, view framed by floor-to-ceiling glass, lived-in but uncluttered premium staging.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a cinematic warm contemporary residential interior: integrated living, dining, and kitchen with natural wood undertones, large-scale stone with organic veining, soft neutral fabrics, filtered daylight, layered embedded lighting, and a landscape view framed by glass. The space should feel calm, tactile, proportioned, lived-in but uncluttered, with refined Brazilian premium interior realism. Avoid sterile showroom emptiness, generic decor, fake marble, plastic CGI, distorted furniture, excessive styling, and fashion-editorial posing.
```

## Continuity Anchors

Warm wood, organic-veined stone, neutral fabric, landscape/view, filtered daylight, layered lighting, calm social flow.
