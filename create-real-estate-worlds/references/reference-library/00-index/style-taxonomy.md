# Style Taxonomy

## Tropical Modern Brasileiro

Use for premium houses, condominiums, resorts, coastal homes, forest homes, and warm-climate projects.

Core cues: horizontal massing, deep shade, indoor-outdoor continuity, warm wood, natural stone, protected glass, tropical landscaping, water reflections, barefoot sophistication, and Brazilian climate logic.

## Vertical Urbano Premium

Use for residential towers, city apartments, rooftops, lobbies, wellness amenities, investor-facing campaigns, and skyline lifestyle.

Core cues: facade rhythm, balconies and terraces, arrival/lobby sequence, rooftop pool or lounge, city skyline, night/blue-hour identity, warm interior glow, concierge/service gestures, privacy, and status.

## Interiores Natural Warmth

Use for interiors that need realistic warmth, material tactility, calm sophistication, and human comfort.

Core cues: natural wood, stone with believable veining, neutral fabric, integrated kitchen/living/dining, filtered daylight, layered lighting, views as part of interior composition, and lived-in restraint.

## Resort / Wellness Residencial

Use for amenities, spas, pool clubs, branded residences, hotels, residential resorts, and lifestyle worlds.

Core cues: water as atmosphere, towel/robe/ceramic/linen/service rituals, calm circulation, garden path, warm restaurant light, shaded lounging, spa quietness, and slow camera movement.
