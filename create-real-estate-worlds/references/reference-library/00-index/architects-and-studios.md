# Architects And Studios

Use these names as architectural research anchors. Do not instruct an image model to copy a living architect's exact style. Instead, extract general architectural principles.

## Brazilian / Tropical / Premium Residential

### Studio MK27 / Marcio Kogan

Useful for: Brazilian modernism continuity, formal simplicity, careful details, horizontal residential compositions, cinematic restraint.

Official source: https://studiomk27.com.br/pb/studiomk27/

### Jacobsen Arquitetura

Useful for: integration between built environment and natural context, tropical architecture, natural materials, lightness, transparent/warm houses.

Official studio source: https://jacobsenarquitetura.com/en/architecture-studio-brazil/

### Bernardes Arquitetura

Useful for: contemporary Brazilian residences, coastal/rural houses, regional reinterpretation, natural materials, courtyards, decks, shaded balconies, hospitality-grade spatial clarity.

Official source examples:

- JCA House: https://www.bernardesarq.com.br/en/projeto/jca-2/
- Casa Terra: https://www.bernardesarq.com.br/projeto/terra/
- Peninsula House: https://www.bernardesarq.com.br/en/projeto/house/
- MTL House: https://www.bernardesarq.com.br/en/projeto/mtl-2/

### Studio Arthur Casas

Useful for: refined Brazilian residential/interior language, multiunit residential, hospitality sensibility, furniture/proportion, warm minimal sophistication.

Official source: https://www.arthurcasas.com/

### Isay Weinfeld

Useful for: restrained luxury, hospitality and residential atmosphere, cinematic spatial sequence, material simplicity.

Official source: https://www.isayweinfeld.com/

## How To Convert Architect Research Into Prompts

Use: Brazilian tropical modern principles, deep shaded terraces, low horizontal massing, natural material hierarchy, indoor-outdoor continuity, restrained cinematic composition, climate-aware glass and shade.

Avoid: "in the exact style of [architect]", "copy [project name]", "same facade as [project]", or "make it look like [specific house]".
