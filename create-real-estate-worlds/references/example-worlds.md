# Example Worlds

Use these as inspiration, not as fixed templates.

## Example 1: Luxo Tropical Brasileiro

**World Name**
Casa Brisa de Pedra

**Logline**
A contemporary tropical home where stone, shade, water, and garden create calm prestige for a high-income Brazilian family.

**Visual DNA**
Low horizontal architecture, travertine or limestone, warm wood ceilings, deep eaves, shaded glass, reflecting pool, tropical garden, linen wardrobe, morning and late-afternoon light, slow gimbal camera, quiet domestic rituals.

**Continuity Anchors**
Cream stone, warm wood soffit, shallow water reflection, large sliding glass panels, dense green garden, off-white linen, black metal details.

**Prompt Seed**

```text
Create a cinematic real estate world for Casa Brisa de Pedra, a contemporary tropical Brazilian residence designed for a high-income family seeking calm prestige and daily connection to garden, water, and shade. Maintain low horizontal architecture, cream natural stone, warm wood ceilings, large shaded glass openings, reflecting water, dense tropical landscaping, natural linen wardrobe, and restrained cinematic camera language. Use realistic Brazilian premium lifestyle, physically plausible materials, natural light, believable scale, candid human behavior, and refined commercial archviz realism. Avoid fantasy, anime, fake luxury, stock-photo posing, plastic CGI, and impossible architecture.
```

## Example 2: Vertical Urbano Premium

**World Name**
Linha Alta

**Logline**
A premium urban tower where arrival, skyline, wellness, and night lighting create a feeling of control over the city.

**Visual DNA**
Vertical facade rhythm, glass and stone podium, warm lobby, concierge gesture, elevator transition, rooftop pool, city skyline, blue-hour exterior, deep interior views, elegant wardrobe, 35mm lifestyle and telephoto skyline shots.

**Continuity Anchors**
Bronze metal, dark stone, warm lobby lighting, skyline beyond glass, rooftop water edge, understated concierge desk, night city reflections.

**Prompt Seed**

```text
Create a coherent cinematic real estate world for Linha Alta, a premium urban residential tower for affluent buyers who want privacy, city command, wellness, and understated status. Preserve the vertical facade rhythm, bronze metal accents, dark stone podium, warm lobby lighting, skyline reflections, rooftop pool edge, and blue-hour city atmosphere. Use refined commercial archviz realism, believable human scale, natural concierge and resident gestures, physically plausible glass reflections, and restrained cinematic camera language. Avoid generic luxury, fake skyline, over-rendered CGI, stock-photo posing, fantasy, and anime styling.
```

## Example 3: Resort Residencial

**World Name**
Fim de Semana Permanente

**Logline**
A residential resort world that turns everyday living into a calm weekend rhythm of pool, garden, gastronomy, spa, and sunset.

**Visual DNA**
Hospitality-inspired arrival, covered walkways, pool club, restaurant terrace, spa corridor, soft towels, garden paths, coastal or countryside light, relaxed wardrobe, slow camera movement, human gestures tied to service and leisure.

**Continuity Anchors**
Pool reflection, textured stone floor, timber pergola, white towels, warm restaurant light, green garden paths, ceramic tableware, soft afternoon sky.

**Prompt Seed**

```text
Create a cinematic residential resort world for Fim de Semana Permanente, a premium real estate lifestyle universe where daily life feels like a quiet weekend of pool, garden, gastronomy, spa, and sunset. Maintain hospitality-inspired architecture, textured stone floors, timber pergolas, reflective pool water, warm restaurant terraces, soft towels, garden paths, relaxed elegant wardrobe, and slow observational camera movement. Use realistic premium lifestyle, natural light, physically plausible materials, candid human behavior, and refined real estate campaign quality. Avoid fake resort cliches, over-saturated sunset, stock-photo posing, fantasy, anime, and plastic CGI.
```
